# Volunteer Management System

This system is an introduction to an idea I have to streamline the process for tracking volunteer events throughout the nation. Due to time constraints, it only has a functional leaderboard at the moment, however, should it continue, it would include a login system, event creations, and volunteer hour submissions and approval workflows.

## Outline

The goal of this system is to centralize volunteer tracking while reducing administrative overhead at the local, region, and national levels.

The system is intended to support the following workflow:

- Squadron Commanders or Chapter Presidents create volunteer events
- Members may submit their own volunteer hours, or officers may submit on their behalf
- Submitted hours require approval by authorized officers (e.g., Commander, President, IMO, Activities Officer)
- Approved hours automatically contribute to real‑time statistics and leaderboards

The system also enables national and regional staff to view up‑to‑date metrics without requiring additional or manual reporting from local units.
